              Pure Signal Card

              Identity → focus → motion. Everything else in this app — your Big 3, your Pure Signal, your workouts, your plan — hangs off these three moves. When you stall, come here.

              1WINSTACK — the identity engine

              Read A → E. Each layer sits on the one before it. Governed top to bottom by Mental Toughness.

              A · IdentityThe narrative — who I am and the story I'm in. If a task doesn't fit the story, that's the block.
              B · StateRecovery across three channels — physical, mental, emotional. Managed, not just "rest."
              C · ConstraintThe layer that outputs the Pure Signal — the one bottleneck. Projects run on a board.
              D · SystemsThe board + calendar. Diagrams are the SOP — draw the process, then scale it, controlled.
              E · IntensityCruise control = controlled aggression — a sustainable high setting, never sprint-and-crash.
              A → E · held together by Mental Toughness

              "The essence of getting the narrative right — in order to drive parity between the self-image and an unleashed capacity to achieve consistent, near-perfect performance — is the calm, accurate, and ultimately automatic separation of fact from fiction, fact from opinion, actual circumstances from magnified obstacles… so that our actions and reactions can be solidly based on truth, not our own or others' opinions." — Jeff Heilman · on Narrative & Identity (WINSTACK layer A)

              3ONE MARK — the procrastination hack
              1. One mark — touch it. Make a single mark. The entry cost is one mark, nothing more.
              2. Remove finish pressure. Finishing is not the contract. Just begin.
              3. Work to the peak. Once moving, ride the session to its natural peak of energy.
              4. Stop & rest. Stop at the peak, rest — then the loop returns to step 1.
              ↻ one mark → no finish pressure → work to the peak → stop & rest → repeat
